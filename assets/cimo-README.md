# ADAPT — Decarbonizing Industrial Steam Supply for CIMO

**ETH Zurich · Energy Now! · October–December 2026 · Ongoing project**

**Energy Systems Modeling · Renewable Energy Integration · Scenario Analysis · Optimization**

## Overview

An ongoing project with **Energy Now! at ETH Zurich**, exploring options to decarbonize industrial steam supply for **CIMO**. The study evaluates combinations of energy sources and system configurations to identify promising solutions under operational constraints.

## Current Work

- Reconstructed a representative annual time series using clustering information provided by CIMO.
- Developed typical-year profiles for **PV, S0, S2, and hydropower**.
- Tested alternative combinations of modeled energy sources and system configurations to compare performance and identify promising options.

## Project Scope

The planned modeling work includes:

- **Model 1 — Portfolio sizing:** compare and size potential supply portfolios.
- **Model 2 — Real-time dispatch:** evaluate operational scheduling and flexibility.
- **Temporal resolution:** six representative weeks at one-minute resolution.

The project context includes approximately **400 GWh/year** of current steam supply, a **2035** scenario with storage, a **130 kt/year** steam gap to cover, and a target of no more than **four six-hour supply interruptions per year**.

## Status

**In progress.** A project report and the two modeling deliverables are scheduled for **14 December 2026**. Findings and final recommendations will be added once the study is complete and approved for public sharing.
