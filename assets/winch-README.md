# Forest Logging Winch — Mechanical Design

**EPFL · Mechanical Engineering · Bachelor Semester 2 (BA2) · Spring 2024 · 3 ECTS**  
**Course:** Mechanical Construction II (*Construction mécanique II*)

**Mechanical Design · Force Analysis · Component Sizing · CAD · Technical Drawings**

## Overview

Design and mechanical dimensioning of a manually operated forest logging winch as part of an EPFL mechanical engineering course project. The work covered the transmission mechanism, cable drum, shafts, bearings, and supporting structure, progressing from load calculations to detailed CAD and manufacturing drawings.

## Engineering Approach

- **Mechanical analysis:** Established force, torque, power, and transmission calculations for the winch mechanism.
- **Component sizing:** Dimensioned gears, shafts, keys, bearings, fasteners, and structural elements.
- **Strength verification:** Evaluated shaft torsion, key shear and bearing stresses, and material suitability.
- **CAD design:** Developed the mechanical assembly and individual components.
- **Technical documentation:** Produced dimensioned engineering drawings, tolerances, and a bill of materials.
