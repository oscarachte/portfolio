# Swiss Solar Boat — Composite Manufacturing

**EPFL · Swiss Solar Boat (MAKE Student Association) · ~1 Academic Year**

**Carbon-Fiber Composites · Wet Layup · Vacuum Infusion · CNC Mold Preparation · Manufacturing**

## Overview

Swiss Solar Boat is an EPFL student engineering association developing a solar- and hydrogen-powered foiling boat for the **Monaco Energy Boat Challenge**.

I participated in the project for approximately one academic year, working on the **manufacturing of carbon-fiber components** for the boat's foil and actuation systems.

![Swiss Solar Boat concept](images/boat-render.png)

## Components and Manufacturing

My work involved manufacturing composite parts for:

- Foil well structures
- Gantry system components
- Torpedo housing
- Rudder structural parts

The manufacturing processes included **carbon-fiber wet layup, vacuum infusion, mold preparation (CNC / G-code), and composite curing**.

## My Contribution

As part of the composite manufacturing team, I contributed to the **hands-on fabrication and preparation of structural and actuation-system components**, from mold preparation and carbon-fiber layup to infusion and curing.

The project provided practical experience in composite manufacturing, workshop processes, and collaboration within a multidisciplinary student engineering team.
