# Raspberry Sorting Gripper

**EPFL · Product Development and Engineering Design · BA5 · 3 ECTS · Team of 5 · 2025**

**Mechanical Design · Prototyping · Arduino · Sensors · System Integration**

![CAD model of the raspberry sorting gripper](images/gripper_cad.jpg)

## Overview

Designed and built an automated robotic gripper to detect, pick and sort raspberries according to **ripeness and size**, as part of an EPFL product development course.

The system combined a mechanical gripping and sorting mechanism with sensors, Arduino-controlled actuators and an integrated detection interface.

## Engineering Approach

- Designed and prototyped the gripper mechanism and housing using CAD, laser cutting and 3D printing.
- Integrated sensors, servomotors and Arduino-based actuation to enable automated handling and sorting.
- Assembled, soldered and tested the physical system, iterating on mechanical and electrical integration.

## My Contributions

- Mechanical design of the gripper.
- Soldering and electronics assembly.
- Manufacturing of the housing.
- System integration and testing.

## Results

- **4th place out of 51 teams** in the EPFL course competition.
- Functional prototype demonstrating an automated **detect → pick → sort** workflow.
- Classification based on both **ripeness and size**.
