# Finite Element Analysis of Rugby and American Football Goalposts

**EPFL · Finite Element Modelling and Simulation · BA6 · 3 ECTS · Team of 4**

**Abaqus · Finite Element Analysis · Structural Mechanics · Meshing**

## Overview

This project investigated the structural behaviour of rugby and American football goalposts using finite element analysis (FEA).

The objective was to develop numerical models of both structures and examine their response to mechanical loading, focusing on **displacements, von Mises stresses, and the influence of mesh resolution**.

## Geometry and Modelling

The two goalpost configurations were modelled based on their different geometries and dimensions.

| Rugby goalpost | American football goalpost |
| --- | --- |
| ![Rugby goalpost geometry](images/rugby.png) | ![American football goalpost geometry](images/nfl.png) |

The Abaqus workflow included geometry creation, material and boundary-condition definition, load application, mesh generation, and post-processing.

## Finite Element Analysis

The numerical investigation covered:

- Structural displacements under applied loading.
- Von Mises stress distributions and local stress concentrations.
- Different loading scenarios and mesh resolutions.
- Comparison of the mechanical response of the two goalpost designs.
