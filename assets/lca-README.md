# G-Shock Sustainability & Life Cycle Assessment

**EPFL · Sustainable Products and Supply Chains (ME-203) · BA4 · 3 ECTS · Spring 2025 · Team of 5**

**Life Cycle Assessment · Sustainable Product Design · Circular Economy · Environmental Impact Modelling**

## Overview

Semester-long group case study investigating how the environmental footprint of a **Casio G-Shock GA-2100** could be reduced through material selection, battery technology and circular product design. We compared a reference watch with a proposed lower-impact alternative using **SimaPro** and **ecoinvent**, and evaluated environmental trade-offs across several impact categories.

The wider group project also considered supply-chain sustainability, stakeholder engagement and implementation strategies.

## Approach

- Assessed the watch's materials, product life cycle and sustainability challenges.
- Proposed a lower-impact design incorporating alternative bracelet materials and a rechargeable battery.
- Built comparative LCA models in **SimaPro**, using **ecoinvent** datasets and the **ReCiPe 2016** impact assessment method.
- Evaluated global warming potential, human toxicity, fossil resource use and mineral resource scarcity.
- Explored battery-technology sensitivity and the limitations of available material datasets.

## Key Results

The **modelled** alternative showed:

| Environmental indicator | Estimated change |
| --- | ---: |
| Global warming potential | **−27%** |
| Human toxicity | **−24%** |
| Fossil resource use | **−21%** |
| Mineral resource scarcity | **−25%** |

These are **comparative academic modelling results**, not verified impacts of a manufactured product. The study used approximations for certain materials, manufacturing processes and life-cycle assumptions.
